// Trade services

What we do with it

We'll normally clone it, not repair it.

Cloning is quicker and cheaper, so it's the first route we look at on almost every unit. Component-level repair is what we turn to when cloning isn't an option — not the default we push you towards.

Cloning: you source the donor unit — and label both

For a clone we need two modules: the faulty one (the patient) and a matching donor unit that you source and send in with it. We copy the data from the patient onto your donor.

Please mark clearly which is which before you post them — a label or a strip of tape on each unit saying "PATIENT" and "DONOR" is enough. Two identical-looking modules in one box with nothing to tell them apart means we have to stop and ask you, which holds the job up.

The donor must match the original exactly on part number, and where possible on build date — manufacturers revise these units in production, and a unit that looks identical from a different revision may not be compatible.

And please don't post the faulty unit on its own expecting a clone — there'll be nothing to clone it onto.
