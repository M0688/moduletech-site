// Questions

Frequently asked

Is remapping safe for my vehicle?

Gains are kept within safe limits for your engine — reliability comes before headline numbers. We're happy to talk through what's appropriate for yours.

Will you remap my car if the engine light is on?

No. If there's an engine management light showing, or a known or suspected fault, it needs sorting first. Remapping asks more of an engine, so tuning a vehicle with an existing problem risks making it worse — and afterwards there's no way to tell what caused what. We'd rather turn the job down than leave you worse off.

What vehicles do you remap?

Cars, vans, trucks, agricultural machinery and plant. If you're unsure whether yours is covered, get in touch and ask.

Will it improve my MPG or my power?

It can do either — but they pull in different directions, so it's worth deciding which matters most to you. Tell us your goal and we'll work to it.

Do I need to bring the vehicle to you?

We offer a mobile service across Watford, Hertfordshire and the surrounding areas, so in most cases we come to you.

How much does it cost?

Remaps start from £200. The final price depends on the vehicle and the work involved, and you'll always have the figure before anything begins.
