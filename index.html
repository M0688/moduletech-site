Questions

Frequently asked

Is remapping safe for my vehicle?

Gains are kept within safe limits for your engine. Reliability comes before headline numbers.

Will you remap it with the engine light on?

No. Any fault needs fixing first.

What vehicles do you remap?

Cars, vans, trucks, plant and agricultural machinery. If you're unsure, ask.

Power or MPG?

A remap can improve either. They pull in different directions, so tell us which matters most.

Do I need to tell my insurer?

Yes. A remap is a modification and should be declared to your insurer.

Do you repair modules?

No. We only do remapping.
