1. Vehicle condition

Remapping asks more of an engine than the manufacturer's settings do. We will only remap a vehicle that is mechanically and electronically sound.

Where such a fault is present, the work will not proceed until the fault has been resolved. It is your responsibility to present the vehicle free of existing faults. Where we attend and the vehicle is found to be unsuitable for remapping, no remapping charge is payable, but we reserve the right to charge for the visit.
