01Nature of our service

We carry out two kinds of work: bench assessment, repair, cloning and data work on automotive electronic control units; and ECU remapping carried out on the vehicle.

Unit work. Our assessment is limited to the unit sent to us and we can only report on what that unit shows on the bench. We do not carry out vehicle-level diagnosis and, unless separately agreed in writing, we do not see, test or work on the vehicle itself.

Remapping. Carried out on the vehicle, and subject to the vehicle condition requirements in clause 8.

02Diagnosis before sending

It is your responsibility to establish that the unit you send is the cause of the fault.

We assume that all external testing has already been carried out before the unit reaches us, including but not limited to:

  • Fuses external to the unit
  • Relays external to the unit
  • Wiring, connectors, earths and supply feeds
  • Related components and sensors

We do not test these items, we are not responsible for testing them, and we accept no liability where a fault is later found to lie in them rather than in the unit sent to us.

06Outcome of work

We undertake to carry out work competently and to a professional standard. We do not guarantee that any work will resolve the fault reported to us, for the reasons set out in clause 2 and because the fault may lie outside the unit sent to us.

In particular:

  • Where a unit is cloned, the procedure transfers the stored data to a replacement board. If the original data is itself corrupt, or if the fault does not lie in the failed board, cloning will not resolve the problem.
  • Where a physical processor transfer is carried out, the outcome depends on the condition of the processor, which cannot be established in advance. If the processor is itself faulty the procedure will not succeed, and the donor board is consumed.
  • Some faults cannot be identified without testing the unit installed in the vehicle with manufacturer-specific equipment.

Our charges cover the work being carried out correctly. They are not contingent on the fault being resolved.

7.1 Attending the vehicle

We are not responsible for investigating engine management lights or other warning lights on a vehicle. An illuminated warning light does not in itself indicate a fault with our work.

Where you ask us to attend and carry out a diagnostic scan and the fault is found to be unrelated to work we have carried out, a fee of £50 is payable.

Where the fault is found to be attributable to our work, no fee is payable and the work will be put right under the warranty above.

08Remapping: vehicle condition

Remapping asks more of an engine than the manufacturer's settings do. We will therefore only remap a vehicle that is mechanically and electronically sound.

We will not remap a vehicle with an illuminated engine management light, stored fault codes, or a known or suspected mechanical or electronic fault.

Where such a fault is present, the work will not proceed until the fault has been resolved. This is not negotiable: remapping a vehicle with an existing fault risks worsening it, and makes it impossible to establish afterwards what caused any subsequent problem.

It is your responsibility to present the vehicle free of existing faults. Where we attend and the vehicle is found to be unsuitable for remapping, no remapping charge is payable, but we reserve the right to charge for the visit.
